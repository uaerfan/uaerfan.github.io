Biography

I am a PhD student and Research Assistant in the Department of Computer Science at The University of Alabama. My research focuses on software engineering, cybersecurity, formal verification, large language models for code, software testing, and vulnerability analysis.

Before beginning my PhD, I served as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Barishal, Bangladesh. My academic experience also includes service as Department Head, Student Advisor, Project Coordinator, and Lecturer, alongside extensive undergraduate and graduate teaching.
